# History archive plan

Generated by `scripts/emit-cutover.js` from `output/inventory/`. Owner: **Data load agent**, reviewed at that agent's gate.

Salesforce deletes some history after a fixed time. From week one of the engagement a scheduled job exports each source incrementally (only rows newer than the last run) into an append-only archive, so nothing expires during the project. At go-live the archive loads into the target's history tables, which keep history without a time limit.

| Source | Salesforce keeps | Reaches back to today | Archive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Field history (171 history tables) | 18 months in the UI, 24 via API; longer only with Field Audit Trail | per table | weekly, by `CreatedDate` watermark |
| Setup Audit Trail (configuration changes) | 180 days | 2026-09-13 | weekly |
| Login history | 6 months | 2026-09-13 | weekly |
| Recycle bin (deleted records) | 15 days | — | daily, `queryAll` where `IsDeleted = true` |
| Event Monitoring logs | 1 day, or 30 with Shield | — | daily, if licensed |

| Decision | Value |
|---|---|
| Retention required by compliance (years, per source) | _to confirm with the client_ |
| History older than today's reach: accepted as not available | _to confirm with the client_ |
| Fields never tracked: changes were not recorded and cannot be recovered | noted |
