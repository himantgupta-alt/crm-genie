# Credentials and integrations checklist

Generated by `scripts/emit-cutover.js` from `output/inventory/`. Owner: **Security agent**, reviewed at that agent's gate.

Passwords, tokens and keys are never exported by Salesforce. Users move to single sign-on (identity provider over SAML or OpenID Connect; users matched by Federation ID) or reset at go-live. Every integration gets a new credential from its owner, stored in Azure Key Vault, tested, then switched on a fixed date. Systems that call into Salesforce are found from login history and kept running through a Salesforce-compatible API until they move.
